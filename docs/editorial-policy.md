# Editorial Policy

## Editorial Mission

AniManga Hub publishes safe, non-explicit, useful anime and manga content that helps users discover titles, understand themes, find legal platforms, and participate in fandom responsibly.

## Review Standards

Reviews should be:

- Honest.
- Non-explicit.
- Spoiler-conscious.
- Useful for viewers/readers.
- Clear about age ratings and mature themes.
- Respectful of creators and audiences.
- Free from piracy encouragement.

Reviews may discuss plot, craft, animation, art, themes, pacing, character writing, music, adaptation quality, and cultural context.

Reviews must not include erotic scene breakdowns, explicit screenshots, or sexualized clickbait.

## Spoiler Policy

Articles must clearly mark spoilers.

Recommended labels:

- Spoiler-free.
- Minor spoilers.
- Major spoilers.
- Ending discussed.

## Mature-Theme Analysis Rules

Allowed mature-theme analysis:

- Age ratings.
- Censorship.
- Viewer discretion.
- Consent in storytelling.
- Fan-service as a trope.
- Violence, grief, trauma, and social themes.
- Cultural context.
- Platform classification.
- Indian legal/cultural context.

Not allowed:

- Explicit sexual descriptions.
- Erotic scene breakdowns.
- Suggestive thumbnails.
- Sexual keyword targeting.
- Hentai indexing.
- Adult redirects.
- Sexualized minor-coded content.

## Legal Platform Link Rules

Editorial content may link only to official/legal platforms, such as Crunchyroll, Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+ Hotstar where applicable, official YouTube channels, Viz, Manga Plus, BookWalker, Kodansha, publisher/studio pages, and official storefronts.

Do not link to unofficial streaming sites, piracy mirrors, torrents, illegal manga readers, hentai sites, adult-video sites, or adult redirects.

## Affiliate Disclosure Rules

Affiliate or sponsored links must be clearly disclosed.

Do not use adult affiliate programs or piracy traffic monetization.

## Image Attribution Rules

Every article image should track:

- Source.
- Permission/license basis.
- Alt text.
- Credit line where required.
- Whether it is official promotional material, owned artwork, licensed stock, or user-submitted with permission.

Do not use pirated manga pages, explicit images, or suggestive thumbnails.

## No Piracy / No Adult Redirect Rule

Editorial content must not teach users how to pirate, list piracy sites, link to illegal readers or streaming mirrors, redirect to adult anime videos, index explicit hentai metadata, or monetize adult/piracy traffic.

## Corrections Policy

AniManga Hub should correct factual errors promptly and log the article, error, correction made, date, editor/author, and whether the correction affects legal/platform availability.

## Legal Review Note

Before launch, editorial policy, affiliate disclosures, and content rules should be reviewed by a qualified Indian cyber-law lawyer.
