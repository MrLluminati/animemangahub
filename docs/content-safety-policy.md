# Content Safety Policy

## Purpose

AniManga Wire must remain a legal, non-explicit anime and manga culture, catalog, review, and legal discovery platform.

It must not become a hentai website, pornographic anime website, piracy website, torrent index, adult-video redirect site, or explicit-content indexing platform.

The project owner is based in India. Indian legal and cyber-law risk must be treated seriously. Foreign hosting, foreign incorporation, or India geo-blocking must not be treated as a complete risk shield.

## Allowed Content

Allowed content includes:

- Anime and manga catalog metadata.
- Legal watch/read guides.
- Anime and manga reviews.
- Legal streaming and manga-reading platform guides.
- Anime industry news.
- Censorship analysis.
- Age-rating and media-literacy commentary.
- Non-explicit mature-theme commentary.
- Cosplay safety and privacy guides.
- Fan convention guides.
- Creator interviews.
- Studio, author, publisher, and platform information.
- Future safe creator/vendor directory listings.

## Restricted Non-Explicit Mature-Theme Content

Restricted mature-theme content may discuss:

- Age ratings.
- Censorship.
- Viewer discretion.
- Fan-service as a trope.
- Consent in storytelling.
- Violence, grief, trauma, and social themes.
- Cultural context.
- Platform classification.
- Indian legal/cultural context.

Rules:

- Keep commentary analytical, educational, and non-erotic.
- Do not include explicit images, erotic scene breakdowns, or sexualized framing.
- Do not use explicit SEO terms, tags, slugs, or thumbnails.
- Use safe labels such as `MATURE_THEMES_NON_EXPLICIT` or `RESTRICTED_DISCUSSION`.

## Absolute Prohibited Content

AniManga Wire must not host, link, index, scrape, monetize, or promote:

- Hentai or pornographic anime content.
- Hentai videos.
- Pornographic anime embeds.
- Adult anime video redirects.
- Explicit anime thumbnails.
- Explicit anime screenshots.
- Explicit hentai metadata.
- Piracy links.
- Torrent links.
- Illegal anime streaming sites.
- Illegal manga readers.
- Adult-site affiliate links.
- Adult ad networks.
- NSFW creator commissions.
- Loli or shota content.
- Sexualized schoolgirl/schoolboy content.
- Teen-coded sexual content.
- Childlike or ambiguous-age characters in sexual contexts.
- Incest.
- Rape.
- Coercion.
- Non-consensual sexual themes.
- Bestiality.
- Abuse-themed sexual content.
- AI-generated sexual content involving youthful-looking characters.
- Any sexualization of minors, real or fictional.

## Image and Thumbnail Rules

Allowed images:

- Official key art where legally usable.
- Platform-approved promotional images.
- User-safe, non-explicit editorial images.
- Properly attributed creator images where permission or license allows use.

Disallowed images:

- Explicit screenshots.
- Nudity or sexualized thumbnails.
- Cropped suggestive images designed for clicks.
- Minor-coded sexualized imagery.
- Hentai or pornographic imagery.
- Pirated scan images.
- Unlicensed manga pages.

## Tag and SEO Rules

Do not use public-facing tags, categories, slugs, page titles, or SEO metadata such as:

- hentai
- NSFW
- porn
- explicit
- sexy
- hot anime girls
- loli
- shota
- schoolgirl sexualized terms

Use safe vocabulary:

- age ratings
- censorship
- media literacy
- mature themes
- viewer discretion
- legal streaming
- legal manga reading
- fandom safety
- cosplay safety

## AI-Generated Image and Content Rules

AI-generated content must not depict or describe sexualized minors, youthful-looking sexualized characters, explicit sexual content, non-consensual sexual content, or prohibited fetish/abuse content.

AI-generated images must not be used to create suggestive thumbnails or imitate copyrighted anime/manga art in a way that creates avoidable legal or brand risk.

## User-Generated Content Rules

Future UGC must be moderated before broad rollout.

Required future controls:

- Report button.
- Moderation queue.
- Spoiler flags.
- Mature-theme label.
- Admin actions.
- Rate limiting.
- Anti-spam controls.
- Audit logs.

## Escalation and Takedown Workflow

1. Receive report or detect violation.
2. Hide or quarantine content if risk is high.
3. Review against this policy.
4. Remove prohibited content.
5. Log moderation action.
6. Warn, suspend, or ban user where applicable.
7. Escalate child-safety or serious legal issues immediately.
8. Preserve internal logs for legal review where appropriate.
9. Consult qualified legal counsel for serious or ambiguous cases.

## Indian Compliance Note

Because the project owner is based in India, compliance review must consider Indian law, intermediary/platform obligations, child-safety obligations, obscenity risk, IT/cyber-law duties, consumer disclosure rules, and advertising/affiliate disclosure issues.

Before launch, this policy should be reviewed by a qualified Indian cyber-law lawyer.
