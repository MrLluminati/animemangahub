# Moderation Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines the future moderation framework for AniManga Hub user-generated content, reviews, comments, reports, marketplace listings, and creator profiles.

Public comments and open community features should not launch until moderation tooling exists.

## Prohibited UGC

Users must not post:

- Piracy links.
- Torrent links.
- Illegal streaming links.
- Illegal manga reader links.
- Adult anime video redirects.
- Hentai or pornographic content.
- Explicit images.
- Explicit sexual descriptions.
- Loli or shota content.
- Sexualized minor-coded content.
- Schoolgirl/schoolboy sexualized content.
- Teen-coded sexual content.
- Incest, rape, coercion, non-consensual sexual themes, bestiality, or abuse-themed sexual content.
- Harassment.
- Hate speech.
- Doxxing.
- Spam.
- Malware or phishing links.
- Counterfeit goods.
- Pirated goods.
- NSFW commissions.

## Report Categories

Future report categories should include:

- Piracy link.
- Explicit content.
- Child-safety violation.
- Sexualized minor-coded content.
- Harassment or hate.
- Spoiler issue.
- Spam or scam.
- Illegal marketplace listing.
- Copyright/IP concern.
- Misinformation or false platform availability.
- Other policy violation.

## Admin Actions

Moderators/admins should be able to:

- Hide content.
- Remove content.
- Mark content as reviewed.
- Add spoiler labels.
- Add mature-theme labels.
- Edit unsafe metadata where legally permitted.
- Warn users.
- Temporarily suspend users.
- Permanently ban users.
- Lock threads.
- Disable listings.
- Escalate for legal review.
- Export/report audit logs.

## Warning, Suspension, and Ban System

Suggested enforcement ladder:

1. No action / report rejected.
2. Soft warning.
3. Formal warning.
4. Temporary content restriction.
5. Temporary suspension.
6. Permanent ban.

Immediate suspension or ban is appropriate for child-safety violations, sexualized minor-coded content, explicit prohibited content, piracy spam, malware/phishing, serious harassment, or threats.

## Escalation Categories

Escalate immediately for child-safety risk, sexual content involving minors or minor-coded characters, non-consensual or abuse-themed sexual content, credible threats, doxxing, legal notices, copyright takedown requests, law-enforcement requests, or high-risk Indian compliance concerns.

## Child-Safety Zero-Tolerance Policy

AniManga Hub has zero tolerance for sexualization of minors, real or fictional, including youthful-looking, childlike, ambiguous-age, school-coded, loli, shota, or teen-coded sexual content.

Such content must be removed or quarantined immediately and escalated for review.

## Appeals Process

Future moderation system should allow users to appeal enforcement actions.

Appeals should include user ID, content ID, enforcement action, user explanation, moderator review result, final decision, timestamp, and reviewer ID.

Appeals should not restore content that violates child-safety or strict prohibited-content rules.

## Logging Requirements

Moderation actions should log actor/admin ID, user/content affected, policy category, action taken, reason, timestamp, appeal status, and legal escalation flag where applicable.

## Launch Requirement

Do not launch public comments, public forums, open marketplace submissions, or unreviewed creator listings until moderation queue, reporting, admin actions, and audit logs are implemented.
