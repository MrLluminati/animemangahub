# Legal Watch/Read Link Policy

## Purpose

AniManga Hub helps users find where anime and manga are legally available. It must not become a piracy directory, torrent index, adult redirect site, or illegal reader guide.

## Official and Legal Platforms Only

Allowed links include official or lawful sources such as:

- Crunchyroll.
- Netflix.
- Prime Video.
- Disney+ Hotstar where applicable.
- Official YouTube channels.
- Viz.
- Manga Plus.
- BookWalker.
- Kodansha.
- Publisher websites.
- Studio websites.
- Licensed theatrical/digital storefront pages.
- Lawful retailer pages for physical/digital manga.

## Prohibited Links

Do not link to:

- Piracy sites.
- Torrent sites.
- Unauthorized anime streaming sites.
- Unofficial manga readers.
- Scanlation aggregators where unauthorized.
- Hentai or adult anime video sites.
- Pornographic redirects.
- Adult affiliate pages.
- Link shorteners used to hide unsafe destinations.
- User-submitted mirror links.

## Legal Platform Verification Checklist

Before adding a platform link, verify:

- The platform appears official, licensed, or otherwise lawful.
- The URL points to the title, platform, publisher, official channel, or lawful retailer.
- The platform is not primarily adult or pirated.
- Region availability is marked where possible.
- Affiliate/sponsored status is disclosed where applicable.
- The link does not use deceptive redirects.
- The platform remains active.

## Region Availability Fields

Future link records should support:

- Country/region.
- Platform name.
- URL.
- Availability status.
- Free/paid/subscription/rental/purchase.
- Language/subtitle/dub notes where lawful and available.
- Last reviewed date.
- Reviewer/admin ID.
- Affiliate flag.
- Disclosure text.

## Affiliate Disclosure Requirements

Affiliate links are allowed only for legal platforms and lawful merchandise/book retailers.

Disclosure must be clear and visible.

Do not use affiliate links for adult sites, piracy sites, hentai sites, torrent traffic, or unsafe marketplace listings.

## Link Review Schedule

Links should be reviewed at creation, every 90 days for high-traffic pages, every 180 days for lower-traffic pages, immediately after user reports, and immediately after platform shutdowns, mergers, or licensing changes.

Funimation should not be treated as a current primary streaming provider because its streaming service shut down and consolidated into Crunchyroll.

## User Reports

Users should be able to report broken links, wrong region, piracy/unofficial links, adult redirects, misleading affiliate links, wrong titles, and platform unavailability.

Reports should enter a moderation/review queue before public link changes are made.

## Compliance Note

Before launch, legal-link workflows, affiliate disclosures, and platform availability pages should be reviewed by a qualified Indian cyber-law lawyer.
